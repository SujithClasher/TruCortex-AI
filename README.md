# TruCortex AI — Enterprise Credit Intelligence Platform
**TransUnion CIBIL | Team Credit Cortex**  
*From Enterprise Knowledge to Connected Intelligence*

---

## 🌟 Overview
**TruCortex AI** is an enterprise intelligence platform designed for **TransUnion CIBIL** to unify credit policies and documentation, detect cross-document contradictions before production, and compute real-time change impact across stakeholder departments:
* **Technical Architecture & Data Governance**
* **Credit Operations & Gateway Integration**
* **Commercial Sales**
* **Quality Engineering & UAT Testing**
* **Learning & Development (L&D)**

---

## 🚀 Key Platform Features

1. **Executive Credit Intelligence Dashboard (PM Only):**
   * Real-time surveillance over TransUnion CIBIL credit guidelines and team dependencies.
   * Key performance metrics: *Pending Impact Reviews*, *Active Contradictions*, *Approved Documents*, and *Stakeholder Sign-Offs*.
   * Interactive SVG visual analytics: *Policy Change Velocity* and *Contradiction Breakdown*.
   * Priority Action Queue tracking assigned document owners, severity levels, and SLA deadlines.

2. **Governed RAG & Ask Cortex:**
   * 25+ verified banking and bureau intents with strict **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) pre-filtering** (e.g. Sales personas are blocked from confidential formula weights *before* retrieval).
   * Real-time streaming answers, verified slide citation chips, and deep retrieval trace evaluation.

3. **Change Impact Radar:**
   * High-performance, GPU-accelerated interactive vector radar mapping ripple effects from a modified credit policy (e.g., `SHG CIR Technical Spec v5.0`) to affected documents across departments.
   * Interactive severity nodes (High: Red, Medium: Amber, Low: Teal).
   * Schema Diff Viewer highlighting exact policy modifications (e.g., `DPD_THRESHOLD_DAYS: 90 -> 60`, `MANDATORY: SHG_GROUP_ID`).
   * AI-powered impact rationale streaming.

4. **Semantic Conflict Radar:**
   * Side-by-side contradiction cards comparing current standards against legacy copies (e.g., `CMR 1-10 vs 1-5`) and gateway rule mismatches (`12 vs 6 months seasoning`).
   * One-click AI explanation and direct ticket assignment to document owners.

5. **Document Ingestion & Human-Gated Release (PM Only):**
   * Drag-and-drop document uploader with a 1-click interactive demo shortcut.
   * 5-stage simulated analysis: *Tokenizing schemas* &rarr; *Extracting entities* &rarr; *Executing Impact Radar* &rarr; *Checking contradictions* &rarr; *Drafting Impact Report*.
   * Human gate approval that commits changes and broadcasts targeted alerts to all departmental leads.

6. **Instant Enterprise Search:**
   * Quick search bar (<kbd>/</kbd>) with live suggestions (`CreditVision CMR`, `SHG CIR v5.0`, `60 DPD Threshold`, `Tradeline Seasoning`).
   * Instant keyword matching with highlighted excerpts and 1-click side-drawer inspection.

7. **Simulated Entra ID Single Sign-On (SSO):**
   * Microsoft-style enterprise authentication supporting 4 TransUnion CIBIL personas:
     * **Vikram Shah** — Product Manager (Full Access, Approver Gate)
     * **Rohan Mehta** — UAT Tester (Technical & QA Scope)
     * **Ananya Rao** — Product Analyst (Product & Analysis Scope)
     * **Priya Nair** — Sales Executive (General & Commercial Scope)
